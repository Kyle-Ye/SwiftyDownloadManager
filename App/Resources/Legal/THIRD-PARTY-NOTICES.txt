On macOS, SDMCore exports the current Apple system trust anchors to a private
PEM file for libcurl. iOS does not expose its system root certificates, so the
iOS libcurl backend uses the audited Mozilla-derived CA bundle copied from the
pinned artifact. URLSession always uses Apple's native trust evaluation.
